Android 隱私權政策

寶島飛行 Formosa Flight · Android Privacy Policy
生效日期 Effective date: 2026-10-05

我們如何處理資料

寶島飛行沒有開發者自建的帳號系統，不需要向開發者註冊或登入。音量、亮度、畫質、操作方式等遊戲設定儲存在你的裝置上，不會同步到開發者的伺服器。

Android 版提供選購的 Google Play 訂閱。App 會連線到 Google Play，以查詢可購買的商品、確認訂閱狀態及恢復購買。付款與訂閱由 Google Play 處理；開發者不會取得你的完整信用卡或其他完整付款資料。

Formosa Flight has no developer-operated account system. You do not register or sign in with the developer. Game preferences, such as volume, brightness, graphics quality, and control settings, are stored on your device and are not synced to a developer server.

The Android version offers optional subscriptions through Google Play. The app connects to Google Play to query available products, check subscription status, and restore purchases. Google Play processes payments and subscriptions. The developer does not receive your full credit card details or other complete payment information.

Google Play Billing 技術診斷資料

App 使用的 Google Play Billing 程式庫會自動將技術診斷資料傳送給 Google，例如 Billing 操作結果碼、錯誤或診斷訊息、操作耗時，以及操作成功或失敗的狀態。這些資料用於分析及改善 Billing 服務的穩定性。

這項技術診斷傳輸是 Billing 功能的一部分，使用者無法在 App 內將它關閉。資料會透過 HTTPS 加密傳輸並與 Google 分享。資料由 Google 依其保留、刪除及安全措施處理；詳情請參閱 Google 隱私權政策。開發者沒有自建伺服器接收這些診斷資料。

The Google Play Billing library used by the app automatically sends technical diagnostic data to Google. This may include Billing operation result codes, error or diagnostic messages, operation duration, and success or failure status. This data is used to analyze and improve the reliability of the Billing service.

This diagnostic transfer is a required part of the Billing functionality and cannot be turned off inside the app. The data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS and is shared with Google. Google handles its retention, deletion, and security practices as described in the Google Privacy Policy. The developer does not operate a server that receives this diagnostic data.

我們不提供的功能

寶島飛行沒有第三方廣告、遊戲內聊天、使用者上傳內容或定位功能。開發者不會利用遊戲活動建立廣告檔案，也不會出售個人資料。

Formosa Flight has no third-party advertising, in-game chat, user-uploaded content, or location features. The developer does not use gameplay activity to build advertising profiles and does not sell personal data.

兒童隱私

寶島飛行適合全年齡遊玩，包括兒童。App 不要求兒童提供姓名、電子郵件、位置或其他個人聯絡資料。免費飛行內容不需要訂閱；未成年人如需購買訂閱，應由家長或監護人透過 Google Play 處理。

Google Play Billing 的必要技術診斷可能在 App 查詢商品或訂閱狀態時自動傳送，無論使用者年齡為何。其處理方式如本政策「Google Play Billing 技術診斷資料」一節所述。

Formosa Flight is suitable for all ages, including children. The app does not ask children to provide a name, email address, location, or other personal contact information. Free flight content does not require a subscription. A parent or guardian should handle any subscription purchase for a minor through Google Play.

Required Google Play Billing diagnostics may be sent automatically when the app checks product or subscription status, regardless of the user's age. This processing is described in the “Google Play Billing diagnostics” section above.

清除本機資料與管理訂閱

你可以在 Android 系統設定中清除 App 資料，或移除 App，以清除裝置上的遊戲設定。

清除 App 資料或移除 App 不會取消 Google Play 訂閱。若要取消或管理訂閱，請另外在 Google Play 的「付款與訂閱」中操作。Google Play 保存的交易與訂閱資料由 Google 管理，清除本機遊戲資料不會刪除這些紀錄。

You can clear the app's data in Android system settings or uninstall the app to remove game settings stored on the device.

Clearing app data or uninstalling the app does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Subscriptions must be managed separately under “Payments & subscriptions” in Google Play. Google manages transaction and subscription records held by Google; clearing local game data does not delete those records.
